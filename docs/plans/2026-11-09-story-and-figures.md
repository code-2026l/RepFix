# Story and figure revision (local only)

The paper's central claim is a compatibility failure: continued representations
can support the primary task and improve a real auxiliary objective while an
existing consumer fails. Scale is an isolated, repairable channel within that
failure, not a complete model of learned drift. Generalization requires measured
boundaries, including the near-null waveform CNN and the head-state factorial.

## Main evidence order

1. A new opening figure defines the current head, retained consumer and repaired
   consumer, then compares the controlled variance intervention and the real HAR
   reconstruction intervention on the full test set. Show every seed and SD.
2. Paired trajectories establish the continuation effect; the fixed hierarchy
   distinguishes scalar correction from richer alignment. Preserve paired-anchor
   requirements. Post-hoc input maps do not change the encoder or auxiliary head.
3. Pure scaling and its exact affine boundaries explain a sufficient mechanism.
   Move the imposed scale sweep to the readout appendix, rather than presenting
   it as the strongest empirical evidence for a learned trajectory.
4. Signed update audits explain why task-gradient relations and unsigned probes
   do not establish this consumer's validity. Keep retrospective versus future
   guarantees explicit. Keep standard disagreement control a baseline.

- Inspect complete rendered pages as well as figure previews; retain nine actual

- Design references: the public author PDFs of Learning Dynamics of LLM
  Finetuning (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.10490) and Visual Autoregressive Modeling
  (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.02905), identified through the official ICLR 2025 and
  NeurIPS 2024 award announcements. Use their clear evidence hierarchy and
  compact explanatory captions as references; copy no assets or data.

## Limits retained

No award prediction; no universal scalar model; no established primary-task
gain; no claim that all real auxiliaries or CNNs fail. The head-state factorial
does not reproduce the historical severe collapse. All 45 RadioML joint
endpoints abstain under the present unlabeled bound. No new training after the
predeclared factorial. No GitHub/OpenReview changes.
