# Remaining manuscript gaps after the compiled-interface round

This audit guides the newly authorized nonlinear-channel study. It does not
claim that a particular experiment, figure style or review response predicts
an award. The preceding PDF has nine main pages and 62 total pages.

1. **Useful adaptation versus compatibility at a realistic cost.** The
   reference-anchored SNR control retains real auxiliary primary benefit but
   costs 2.37x in its declared CPU path. The cheap frozen-trunk control preserves
   logits but loses the useful SNR effect. Its matched unconstrained freezing
   control shows that the dominant measured loss is freezing. A trainable
   nonlinear channel is a testable next hypothesis, with its capacity and cost
   exposed rather than hidden by the phrase "one encoder."

2. **The positive result's external scope is still narrow.** Ten new seeds
   quantify seed/mask variation on one prepared split and recipe. They are not
   new populations or datasets. The strong waveform CNN is a near-null
   reconstruction boundary, not a positive SNR transfer confirmation. A new
   HAR 1%-label reconstruction regime is declared in this round's validation
   grid; results in that regime will not be pooled with prior HAR fractions.
   If it does not qualify, report the failed cross-task attempt plainly.

3. **Intervention is not yet an online warning policy.** The exact controls
   operate from the start. Logged anchor disagreements are diagnostics, not
   evidence of warning lead time or a certified future optimizer trajectory.
   Keep Algorithm 1's retrospective, applied-step and finite-corpus scopes
   distinct. This round does not silently upgrade that operational claim.

4. **Algorithmic novelty is vulnerable to overstatement.** Backward-compatible
   learning, orthogonal/nullspace updates, side-tuning and parameter merging
   are established. The paper's defensible center is its retained-consumer
   failure diagnosis, paired causal controls, signed-probe limitations and
   explicit compatibility/adaptation/cost comparisons. A nonlinear-channel
   success would be a constructive control, not evidence of inventing a new
   family of adaptation algorithms. Acknowledged primary sources include
   Shen et al. (CVPR 2020), Farajtabar et al. (AISTATS 2020), Zhang et al.
   (ECCV 2020), Hu et al. (ICLR 2022) and Pham et al. (2026).

5. **Healthy affine consumers do not cover arbitrary deployed consumers.**
   The exact identity needs a retained deterministic reference path/weights,
   fixed normalized affine consumer and available nullspace. Multiple heads
   can exhaust that nullspace. Nonlinear consumers, preprocessing changes,
   stochastic evaluation and finite-precision implementations need separate
   treatment. The paper should preserve those assumptions beside its claims.

6. **Protocol changes and manuscript sprawl can obscure the evidence.**
   The fp32, mixed-precision and proposed all-double encoder studies are
   separate protocols. New seed effects cannot be attributed solely to an
   architecture or precision difference across studies. Confirmatory comparisons
   must be paired inside a study. Keep the main narrative on three axes:
   retained outputs, actual task/auxiliary utility and deployment cost. Use
   the appendix for complete controls and preserve the earlier negative evidence.

The current round fixes its grid, validation selection, new confirmation seeds,
noninferiority margin, real-auxiliary MSE check and CPU budget before development.
The causal control is a matched unconstrained nonlinear side path. Frozen-base
weights embedded in a wider deployed encoder and dense zero blocks count toward
both parameter/memory and runtime reporting. Full target failure remains a
possible and reportable outcome.

## Outcome of this declared round

Both tasks fail the development gate, so no scientific test confirmation is
launched. The new evidence verifies compatible nonlinear changes and dense
fusion; it does not close the joint utility/cost gap. A matched side with its
own nonlinear prefix frozen was not tested, so altered prefix weights alone
do not establish a causal benefit from prefix learning. The matched U/T control
isolates the output constraint at equal added capacity. Full records, the
exact-count zero-boundary correction and all 87 independently replayed
validation consumers are preserved in the new revision report.
