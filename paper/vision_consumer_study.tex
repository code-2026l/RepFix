\subsection{A Distinct Vision Consumer Under Primary-Task Updates}\label{app:vision_consumer}

The final fixed screen separates the current task from the legacy task.
An ImageNet-V1 ResNet-18 emits $512$ features for CIFAR-100 at $64\times64$.
The current head predicts $100$ fine classes and the legacy head predicts
$20$ coarse classes~\citep{krizhevsky2009cifar}. Both healthy heads fit the same
$20\%$ labeled subset. Coarse labels are excluded from continuation and OLS
fitting; they remain available to an explicitly supervised refit. The coarse label is derivable from the fine label.

Seed $1800$ partitions the official training split into $42{,}952$ fitting,
$2{,}048$ isolated anchor and $5{,}000$ validation inputs. Healthy features are
cached once; running BN statistics remain frozen. The published one-epoch
screen uses float32, batch $128$, SGD momentum $0.9$, weight decay
$5\cdot10^{-4}$, encoder learning rate $10^{-3}$ and head/map rate $10^{-2}$.
All arms clone the same healthy encoder and affine heads and share future
minibatches. F freezes the encoder; U updates it without protection. P learns
a coarse head by healthy-logit MSE at coefficients $0.1,1,10$; B learns a full
feature map at the same coefficients. OLS uses all paired fitting features
and healthy logits. The supervised refit uses the original coarse labeled
subset. No auxiliary loss or augmentation is added: the question is transfer
of the interface contract under a primary-task update.

U fails the required two-point current-task gain over F: its difference is
$-3.78$ points. P ($0.1$) improves over both continuation baselines but changes
$44.56\%$ of coarse decisions, above the $5\%$ budget. OLS improves coarse
accuracy while leaving $41.20\%$ of decisions changed. The stronger P
coefficients $1$ and $10$ produce nonfinite losses. The bounded queue stops
after these two failures: B ($0.1$) is interrupted, and B ($1,10$) are never
launched. The B branches remain unmeasured. The failed utility gate ends the screen
before official test evaluation. The saved complete
endpoints undergo independent checkpoint/reader replay; failure logs and queue
states are retained separately. This initial full-backbone recipe yields no positive utility/compatibility result.
