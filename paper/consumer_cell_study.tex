\subsection{Consumer-Cell Development Study}\label{app:consumer_cell_study}

The preceding geometry motivated a separately planned loss comparison
before shared replay. Its purpose was to preserve specified decisions
without forcing every logit to equal its healthy value. All choices use
healthy predictions and fitting inputs; selection never uses new labels
for the old consumer. The class and two fixed confidence-threshold outputs
define the primary contract. A separately declared class-only ablation
removes confidence constraints from training but still scores all consumers.
Standard distillation and full-feature alignment are retained comparators.

Stage A freezes saved development encoders for seeds $800$--$802$ on both
tasks. It fits affine, diagonal-quadratic and width-$32$ residual readers
with temperature-$1/2/4$ KL, centered logit MSE, consumer-cell KL and
consumer-cell log-odds objectives, plus OLS: $114$ endpoints. Adam uses
learning rate $10^{-3}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$, batch $1024$, $60$ epochs
and a common gradient-norm cap of $5$. Neither task finds a candidate
meeting the $2.5\%$ development disagreement limit for all three consumers.

Stage B jointly continues the original healthy encoder and Adam state.
Each of the six records includes detached/joint controls, conditioned raw
logit distillation, full-feature alignment, and four objectives at four
fixed coefficients with affine/residual readers: $216$ endpoints. Primary
label fractions, auxiliary doses, $30/25$ epochs and batch $256$ remain
fixed. All arms use the same gradient cap and future orders. The separate
class-only study adds $96$ endpoints. Complete grids and immutable plans
are included in the artifact; no test scoring enters development.

Neither task passes the primary three-consumer gate. The class-only HAR
cell log-odds candidate is formally eligible, but is development-dominated
by a standard temperature-$1$, coefficient-$1$ KL control: reconstruction
ratios are $0.633$ versus $0.626$, primary differences $-0.272$ versus
$-0.136$ points, and worst class disagreements $2.109\%$ versus $2.041\%$.
We retain that eligibility and apply an additional, explicitly retrospective
futility stop before any new test confirmation. SNR has no eligible cell
candidate. The proposed cell losses therefore add no confirmed training advantage
over the standard controls. Independent SLSQP, envelope-gradient and serving
checks verify their numerical implementation.
